Clear ESP32 breadboard attachment map

Match each numbered badge on the diagram to the table. The table tells you the exact pin or breadboard hole to attach. Use ESP32 silkscreen labels, not pin counting, because DevKit boards vary.

Important: HC-SR04 ECHO is 5 V. Do not wire ECHO straight to the ESP32. Use the 1k/2k divider: raw ECHO column B32 goes through 1k to column 36, GPIO39/VN reads column C36, and 2k goes from D36 to ground.
