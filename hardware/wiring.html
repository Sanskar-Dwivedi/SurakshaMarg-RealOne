Hardware build sheet

Bench governor — wiring

A hardware mirror of the software governor. Every veto is enforced again by a microcontroller that does not trust the host, so two independent layers must agree before the transducer is energised — and either can stop it alone.

Read this before opening Tinkercad

Tinkercad Circuits cannot simulate an ESP32. It supports Arduino Uno, ATtiny and micro:bit only. The “ESP32” results you find there are 3D models, not circuits. Build the Uno diagram in Tinkercad; use Wokwi for the ESP32, or just flash your real board.

Two ESP32 gotchas that will cost you an evening

GPIO35 has no internal pull-up. Add a 10 kΩ resistor from pin 35 to 3V3 or the E-stop input floats and latches at random. The supplied diagram.json already includes it.

ADC pins are not 5 V tolerant. The potentiometer goes to 3V3, not 5V.

Build it in about fifteen minutes

  1. tinkercad.com → Circuits → Create new Circuit.
  2. Drag in: Arduino Uno R3, breadboard, HC-SR04, piezo, 4 LEDs, 4× 220Ω, 3 pushbuttons, potentiometer.
  3. Wire it per the diagram above. Colours in the picture match the colours in the table.
  4. Code → Text (not Blocks). Paste gaukavach_uno.ino.
  5. Start Simulation, open the Serial Monitor.
  6. Drag the slider on the HC-SR04 — that is your approaching animal.

The demo — five moves, ninety seconds

  1. Sensor to ~80 cm. Green LED. Serial: PERMITTED @ 28.6 m, carrier 25.0 kHz.
  2. Hold PERSON. Emission stops mid-burst, red LED: “a person is inside the exposure cone”. Release — it does not resume, because the quiet period is now running.
  3. Turn the pot past 3. “group large enough that a startle could cascade”. A flock is a dispatch problem, not an acoustic one.
  4. Sensor under ~34 cm. “already at the carriageway — fleeing would cross it”. The system will not push an animal across the road.
  5. Let it run. After three attempts the amber LED latches: ESCALATED. It gives up rather than getting louder.

Then press E-STOP. Nothing clears it but a reset.

What this rig does not prove

Any decibel level. No calibrated ultrasonic microphone is attached, so an SPL claim about this rig would be fabricated. Both sketches print that warning at boot.

Anything about cattle. No animal is involved. And if your transducer is a 40 kHz ranging element, note that 40 kHz sits above the 35 kHz cattle audiogram endpoint — it is the wrong part, standing in for the signal chain only.
